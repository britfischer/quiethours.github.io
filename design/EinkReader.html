The art of the weekly reset

Most of us treat Sunday evening like a cleanup crew. We sweep up the week, file what we can, and feel vaguely behind.

A reset is not a cleanup. It is a short conversation with next week. It asks three questions, and it should take less time than an episode of anything.

First: what am I carrying forward? Pick one thing. Not five. The point is to choose, and to let the rest go without guilt.
