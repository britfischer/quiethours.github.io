Page headings are 32. Display 44 is the Home greeting and the two onboarding screens only, and 40 is the title of a piece of content you have opened. Empty states are the smallest heading in the system: serif 600 at 20, bold enough to anchor the block without shouting. One size sits above the scale and is used once: 52 for the sign-in headline. A plain serif line at 18 in muted ink is the only other extra, for an ending sentence.

Open content follows one header pattern whatever it is: a small meta or type line, then the title in serif 400 at 40 (30 on phone), then at most one muted serif line at 20, then the image, player or body. Article, podcast, video and show pages all use it. No italics anywhere. The one flourish is a phrase in red inside a greeting or an ending line, set upright, never a whole sentence. Phone drops one step: display 30, section 24, row title 17, meta 12.

Navigation & list controls

UnseenSeenAll

Everything sits on its own above the Folders list, not inside it: it is a fixed destination and can't be renamed or deleted, so it never carries the folder ⋯ menu, and it always shows one scroll of everything that has come in. Folders are labels on top of that: a source can sit in several or none, and folder changes never take anything out of Everything. Pill nav is the app's five places. Underline tabs are state only, never type. Every list reads Group by, Sort, Filter, in that order, Filter last on the right. Type, source and length live inside Filter. Group by defaults to None, Sort to Newest first, and both remember your last choice per page. The whole set sits in one row of its own directly under the page heading, never in the heading row: tabs on the left, Group by, Sort, Filter on the right, same spot on every screen.

Left to right: new dot (7px red, or an empty 7px spacer when seen), thumbnail, title with the red arrow attached to the last word, meta line starting with the type badge, hover actions, then ⋯. The whole row is clickable. Badges: Article and Recipe on tint 1, Video solid olive with white text, Podcast on tint 2. Titles clamp at two lines, about 140 characters, cut at a word with "…" before the arrow. A row's shape never changes with its state: a badge or button can appear, but the folder chip and ⋯ are always there.

Rules the boards follow

Dividers2px above a section heading, 1px between rows, one color (#D5D0C3), and never after the last row in a list.
EndingsEvery list ends with a sentence, not a fade. Nothing offers more content after telling you you're finished.
ConfirmationsInline where the thing happened, with an olive Undo. No toasts, no popups that disappear.
CountsOff by default. No badges on tabs, no numbers on the app icon. Later and unfinished things never create a count.
MenusWhite, 16px radius, 44px items, grouped with 1px separators, destructive last in deep red. No footnotes under items.
Accessibility44px minimum target, visible focus, ink on page is 11:1, muted ink 5.6:1, olive on page 6.9:1, and color is never the only signal: the new dot carries hidden "New" text.
